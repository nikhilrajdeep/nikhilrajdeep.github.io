Research & background

I am a PhD candidate in Soil, Water, and Ecosystem Sciences at the University of Florida, working with Dr. Ebrahim Babaeian.

My doctoral research examines soil moisture, hydraulic soil properties and water fluxes at the field scale. I combine L band microwave radiometry, field measurements, radiative transfer modelling and machine learning to estimate moisture at different soil depths and understand how water moves through Florida’s sandy agricultural soils.

I study the effective depth of microwave emission to improve estimates of moisture in the root zone. I also develop physics guided neural pedotransfer functions for soil water retention and hydraulic properties, alongside methods for downscaling and forecasting SMAP soil moisture. My work includes automated retrieval workflows, an R Shiny application for brightness temperature simulation and soil moisture estimation, and Google Earth Engine tools for monitoring soil moisture and producing cloud free imagery.

Before joining UF, I worked as a Research Scientist at the National Remote Sensing Centre, Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). There, I worked on national land use and land cover mapping, automated mapping workflows, shifting cultivation and soil organic carbon estimation using PRISMA imaging spectroscopy.

My earlier research at IIT Kharagpur explored mangrove species, leaf chlorophyll and carbon stocks using field inventories and AVIRIS NG imagery. At the Defence Terrain Research Laboratory, I developed spectral libraries and models of soil texture and worked on flood monitoring. These experiences continue to shape my interests in agriculture, vegetation dynamics, soil, water and environmental change.

The questions that guide my work

What is my research about?

  1. 01

    Water in agricultural landscapes

    Estimating soil moisture and hydraulic properties, understanding water fluxes, and connecting microwave observations with agricultural water management.

  2. 02

    Vegetation, mangroves and carbon

    Using imaging spectroscopy and field measurements to study species composition, leaf chlorophyll, leaf area index, vegetation condition and mangrove carbon stocks.

  3. 03

    Land, soil and changing environments

    Mapping land cover and shifting cultivation, estimating soil organic carbon and soil texture, and examining floods, surface water and wetland restoration.

  4. 04

    New questions in greenhouse gas emissions

    I am interested in extending my geospatial research to methane and other greenhouse gas emissions across agricultural and wetland landscapes.

03 · Research

What I am working on

Microwave

Root-zone soil moisture from L-band radiometry

A calibrated τ–ω framework and an equilibrium-depth-scaling method that link surface brightness temperature to moisture deeper in the root zone, tested with a UAV L-band radiometer over potato fields at Citra, Florida.

SAR

NISAR soil-moisture validation

Evaluating NISAR L-band GCOV and the L3 soil-moisture products against about 80 in-field probe locations at four depths, using a water-cloud and integral-equation modelling approach.

Soil physics

Physics-informed pedotransfer functions

Neural networks that predict van Genuchten water-retention parameters and hydraulic conductivity from soil properties, with physical constraints and ensemble uncertainty — benchmarked against Rosetta on independent datasets.

Water & crops

Plant-available water forecasting

Forecasting plant-available water for Florida's sandy soils and cropping systems, connecting satellite observables to the agronomic water that crops can actually use.

Optical

Land cover & crop mapping

National LULC from AWiFS / LISS-III and seasonal crop-area mapping with Sentinel-1/2 on Google Earth Engine — the kind of mapping that turns satellite imagery like the globe above into classified maps.

Spectroscopy

Imaging spectroscopy of soil and mangroves

Soil organic carbon from PRISMA and AVIRIS-NG imagery, soil spectral libraries, and hyperspectral chlorophyll estimation in mangroves.
